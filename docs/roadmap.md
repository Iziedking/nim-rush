# Roadmap

NIM RUSH is a daily downhill race inside Nimiq Pay. One city, 1.9 km, 90
seconds. Your wallet signs the run, the server re-simulates it from your input
trace, and the day's top three split a funded Nimiq mainnet pool.

Here is what we want to add over the next few months.

## A run with a job in it

A free run is a descent with a clock on it. We want it to be a delivery: a
payment stuck somewhere down the hill, a depth you have to reach before the
clock runs out, and the choice of whether to spend nitro getting there early or
save it for the section you know is coming. Reaching the fault is the win
condition, not reaching the bottom.

## Drifting worth doing

Gearboxes buy slides and slides feed the tank, but a clean drift does not yet
feel like it paid. We want a slide scored while it is happening, chained
through a corner, and called out the moment it lands - the loop Downhill
Domination gets right, where the reason to take the harder line is that the
harder line is worth more.

## Points you can spend

Career points are a ladder today and nothing else. We want them to convert into
NIM and NIM to buy upgrades - a bigger tank, a fourth gearbox, eventually a
different bike.

Two things this will not do. It will not touch ranked: every daily challenge
stays on one fixed loadout, because a board where the fastest rider is the one
who spent the most is not a board. And nothing converts until there is a funded
treasury behind it, the same rule the daily pool already runs on - a reward
reads as owed until the transfer has been seen on chain.

## More bikes

One frame today. We want a handful, with real differences - a faster bike that
asks more of you in a corner, a heavier one that holds a line through contact -
and more missions per city to give them something to be good at.

## Racing somebody who can fight back

The pack you ride against is recorded, so contact moves you and never them.
That is honest and it is not a race. We want both bikes live at once, where a
shoulder going into a corner costs the other rider too and somebody can be put
out before the line.

## A fuller hill

Three riders share the course with you today. We want that to be seven, and we
want a private lobby to feel like a start gate rather than a queue: everyone
dropping in together, positions changing, somebody you can actually see getting
away from you.

## Split times

A finish time tells you how you did. A split tells you where you lost it. We
want per-sector times against the rider above you, called out as you cross each
gate, so a run ends with a specific thing to fix rather than a number.

## More cities

London and Dubai are built. We want them earned by riding well rather than
unlocked by a release, and a fourth city after that with weather that changes
how the surface behaves.

## A voice on the hill

Recorded commentary that reacts to the ride: the contact you just took, the
gearbox you just spent, the ten seconds you have left. Short lines, spoken by
somebody, not read out by a browser.

## Boards worth climbing

A weekly board alongside the daily one, so a rider who misses a day still has
something to chase, and a season table underneath both.

## Riding on more phones

We test on the devices we own. We want a wider pass across older Android
hardware and a frame budget that holds on all of it, because a race that
stutters is a race nobody comes back to.
